## ✨ The Vision
Most productivity apps feel like a digital whip—stressing you out with overdue dates and red notifications. **Cozy Toolbox** is different. It's designed to be a "sanctuary" for your focus, blending the efficiency of a Pomodoro timer with the emotional reward of a cozy RPG.

### 🛠️ The Toolset
- **✨ Daily Intentions:** Focus on the "Big 3." A minimalist space to set your a-priori goals for the day. Resets every morning.
- **📝 Quest Log:** A gamified To-Do list. Complete tasks to earn XP, level up your "Adventurer" rank, and unlock trophies.
- **⏳ Focus Flow:** A Pomodoro timer that balances deep work with essential breaks, rewarding you with XP for every session completed.
- **📖 Cozy Journal:** A safe space for end-of-day reflection and mood tracking.
- **👤 Profile & Settings:** Track your lifetime focus time and customize the app's theme to match your current mood.

## 🌈 Aesthetic Themes
The app supports multiple "vibes" to keep your environment fresh:
- 🍦 **Cream:** The classic, warm vanilla start.
- ☁️ **Sky:** A light, airy, and focused atmosphere.
- 🌿 **Sage:** An organic, grounding, and calm experience.
- ☀️ **Amber:** A bright, energetic, and warm glow.
